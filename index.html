reChord

Movies that sound like you.

reChord reads your Spotify listening, measures how your music feels (energy, mood, atmosphere, the hours you listen) and recommends films with the same emotional signature. It also forecasts how your music will feel in the coming days, so the right films are ready before you ask.

Interactive mockup. Everything on the phone is clickable, but no real Spotify data is read. Recommendations and forecasts are fixed sample data: the recommender system and the predictive model are not implemented yet.
